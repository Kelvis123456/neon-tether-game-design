# PHASE 2 REPORT: Concept Generation & Critiques

This document details our brainstorming of 30 distinct mobile game concepts, evaluates them critically, and details the selection process for the final game concept.

---

## Concept Catalog (1 to 30)

### Concept 1: Orbitum
* **Description:** Control a small particle orbiting central gravity wells. Tap to break orbit and launch into the next well's gravitational pull.
* **Core Mechanic:** Tap to launch, automatic orbital rotation, gravity attraction.
* **Core Loop:** Enter orbit -> Time the release -> Orbit next node -> Accumulate score.
* **Target Audience:** Casual players who love timing games (Helix Jump, Duet).
* **Originality:** High. Shifts focus from traditional endless runners to orbital transition dynamics.
* **Expected Fun:** 9/10. High satisfying risk-reward of launching at the perfect angle.
* **Technical Difficulty:** Medium (physics vectors and gravity equations).
* **Scalability:** High (new planets, solar wind obstacles, black holes).
* **Strengths:** Simple one-touch controls, satisfying fluid movement.
* **Weaknesses:** Can feel repetitive if orbital speeds don't vary.
* **Commercial Potential:** High. Skin customization (planets, trails) and premium soundscapes.

## Critique & Selection Process

### The Filtering Process
To select our final concept, we evaluated all 30 options against our core criteria:
1. **Adrenaline Hook (Addictiveness):** Does it trigger the "just one more run" psychological response?
2. **One-Handed Playability:** Essential for modern mobile gaming (portrait orientation preferred).
3. **True Mechanical Originality:** Must not feel like a clone of an existing popular title.
4. **Feasibility & Optimization:** Physics must be solid and cheap to compute.

### Top 3 Finalists:

#### 1. Concept 1: Orbitum (Orbital gravity timing)
* **Critique:** Excellent physics timing, but only having one button (launch) limits the choice matrix. The player mostly waits for the angle.

#### 2. Concept 14: NeonSplit (Binary Split-and-Merge)
* **Critique:** The core mechanic is simple: **Hold to split, release to merge**. It splits two connected particles outward to the sides, and merges them back in the center. The movement path is governed by an elastic thread holding them together.
* **Verdict:** Highly original. The cognitive challenge of managing two symmetric spheres around a central divider is immediately engaging. It triggers the optimal zone of flow and frustration.

#### 3. Concept 27: TetherLink (Pendulum Climbing)
* **Critique:** Great physics feel, but swing controls can feel slightly unpredictable for hyper-fast reaction gameplay.

## The Selected Concept: **DualSplit** (Working Title: **Neon Tether**)
We selected **DualSplit** (commercial title: **Neon Tether**) because it hits the sweet spot of easy to learn, difficult to master, high optical juice, rhythmic integration, and clean mobile controls.

### Core Loop:
1. **Dodge:** Player's core (two particles connected by a glowing elastic band) moves forward through a neon tunnel. Tap/Hold splits the twin particles to hug the outer walls; releasing snaps them back to the center.
2. **Collect:** Gather energy nodes in the tunnel. Some are only accessible by splitting wide; some are strictly in the center.
3. **Flow:** Obstacle arrangements form rhythmic sequences syncing with high-energy soundtracks.
4. **Survive:** Dodge central obstacles (by splitting) and wall obstacles (by merging).
