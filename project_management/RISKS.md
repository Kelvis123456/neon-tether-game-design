# RISKS REGISTER: Antigravity Game Project

This document registers potential technical, design, or commercial risks and outlines mitigation strategies.

---

| ID | Description | Impact | Probability | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-001** | Game isn't fun or lacks "hook" | High | Medium | Develop vertical slice early; iterate on core mechanics repeatedly before full coding. |
| **RSK-002** | Platform performance issues (mobile devices) | High | Low | Conduct profiling early; design with low draw-calls and lightweight physics; optimize draw cycles. |
| **RSK-003** | Scope creep (over-complicating systems) | Medium | High | Rely strictly on Phase 4 GDD; ensure every mechanic justifies its footprint. |
| **RSK-004** | Intrusive/Pay-to-Win monetization driving players away | High | Low | Commit strictly to ethical, cosmetic-only monetization. Retain players with pure gameplay and rewarding events. |
